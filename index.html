The same porch, a new century

Judge Horatio Davis built the house in 1899. Albert Blanding, later a general, lived here from 1900. The wraparound porch they knew is the one you’ll have your morning coffee on.

Who it’s for

Made for the weekends that matter

Whether you’re visiting for a game, graduation or a UF family weekend, make yourself at home.

  • Game weekends

    Gather on the porch, grill out back, and catch the early games on 98 inches before you head to the stadium.

  • Graduation

    The graduate, the siblings and the grandparents, all under one roof, eight minutes from the O’Connell Center. Graduation weekends will go first.

  • Family Weekend and move-in

    A comfortable home base while you settle your Gator in, with a porch to recover on afterward.

  • All four years

    Tell us your student’s class year. We’d love to be your home away from home from orientation to commencement.
