127 years on NE Third Street

The same porch, a new century

Historical accounts credit Judge Horatio Davis, twice mayor of Gainesville, with building the house in 1899. General Albert Blanding, the man Camp Blanding is named for, lived here from 1900. Six years later the University of Florida opened its Gainesville campus, and the house has watched every Gator season since.
