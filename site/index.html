Built by a mayor, home to a general

Historical accounts credit Judge Horatio Davis, twice mayor of Gainesville, with building the house in 1899. General Albert Blanding, the man Camp Blanding is named for, lived here from 1900. Six years later the University of Florida opened its Gainesville campus.
