Visitor’s Guide

Our picks for coffee, dinner, drinks and an afternoon out, with the walk or drive from our front door.

Coffee and breakfast

Most of these are an easy walk. Wyatt’s and Maude’s are closest.

  • Wyatt's Coffee

    6 min walk

    Independent cafe downtown: espresso, pour-over and pastries. $$

    202 SE 2nd Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Maude's Classic Cafe

    8 min walk

    Coffee, tea, desserts and sandwiches beside the Hippodrome, with board games and an outdoor terrace. $

    101 SE 2nd Pl, Gainesville, FL

  • Afternoon

    17 min walk

    Brunch spot known for its Dutch baby and shakshuka. Check the days before you go. $$

    231 NW 10th Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Afternoon Coffee

    15 min walk

    Small coffee bar on South Main, handy on the way to Depot Park. $

    619 S Main St, Gainesville, FL

  • Opus Coffee

    20 min walk

    Local roaster's cafe at Innovation Square, between downtown and campus. $

    800 SW 2nd Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Baker Baker

    17 min walk

    Family-run bakeshop at the 4th Avenue Food Park. Mornings only, so go early. $

    407 SW 4th Ave, Gainesville, FL

Lunch and dinner

Downtown’s restaurants are within ten minutes on foot. Book ahead on game and graduation weekends.

  • The Top

    6 min walk

    Downtown New American restaurant since 2000. Dinner nightly and Sunday brunch. $$

    30 N Main St, Gainesville, FL

  • Dragonfly Sushi & Sake

    7 min walk

    Sushi and Japanese small plates. Takes reservations, which helps on big weekends. $$

    201 SE 2nd Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Harry's Seafood Bar & Grille

    7 min walk

    New Orleans-style seafood, downtown since 1994. $$

    110 SE 1st St, Gainesville, FL

  • OAK

    7 min walk

    Southern comfort food with self-serve beer taps and a Friday brunch. $$

    15 SE 1st Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Boca Fiesta

    9 min walk

    Tacos, margaritas and fresh lemonade. Lunch on weekends. $$

    232 SE 1st St, Gainesville, FL

  • Liquid Ginger

    8 min walk

    Asian fusion: pad thai, curries and bento boxes. $$

    101 SE 2nd Pl, Gainesville, FL

  • Kyuramen

    9 min walk

    Ramen and milk tea in the Hyatt Place building. $$

    212 SE 1st St, Gainesville, FL

  • Flaco's Cuban Bakery

    9 min walk

    Cuban sandwiches, arepas and bowls, open late most nights. $

    200 W University Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Satchel's Pizza

    6 min drive

    Local favorite pizza place with famously eclectic decor. Tuesday through Saturday. $$

    1800 NE 23rd Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • The Swamp Restaurant

    4 min drive

    Gator-themed sports restaurant about 20 minutes on foot from the stadium. $$

    1104 SW 2nd Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Mildred's Big City Food

    10 min drive

    Upscale American and seafood since 1994. Book ahead for a graduation dinner. $$$

    3445 W University Ave, Gainesville, FL

Breweries and cocktails

Cocktail bars a few blocks away, and three local breweries.

  • Cry Baby's

    7 min walk

    Cocktail bar with food on the corner of University and Main.

    1 W University Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Kin Cocktail Bar

    7 min walk

    Craft cocktail lounge downtown. Closed Mondays.

    9 W University Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • The Bull

    8 min walk

    Beer and wine pub with live music most nights.

    18 SW 1st Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Cypress & Grove Brewing

    17 min walk

    Brewery in a former ice factory, open daily from 2 pm.

    1001 NW 4th St, Gainesville, FL

  • First Magnitude Brewing

    4 min drive

    Brewery with a big beer garden and food trucks, beside the Hawthorne Trail.

    1220 SE Veitch St, Gainesville, FL

  • Swamp Head Brewery

    14 min drive

    Gainesville's original craft brewery (2008), with a large outdoor area and food trucks.

    3650 SW 42nd Ave, Gainesville, FL

Things to do

Museums, theater and Florida outdoors, from a four-minute walk to a twenty-minute drive.

  • Union Street Farmers Market

    5 min walk

    Wednesdays 4 to 7 pm, rain or shine. Bring cash.

    Bo Diddley Plaza, 111 E University Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Hippodrome Theatre

    9 min walk

    Professional theater in the old federal building.

    25 SE 2nd Pl, Gainesville, FL

  • Matheson History Museum

    6 min walk

    Free museum of local history, Wednesday through Saturday.

    513 E University Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Historic Thomas Center

    4 min walk

    1910s Mediterranean Revival hotel turned city galleries, with gardens to wander.

    302 NE 6th Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Depot Park

    19 min walk

    City park with a big playground, a splash pad, trails and ponds.

    874 SE 4th St, Gainesville, FL

  • Cade Museum

    18 min walk

    Hands-on science and invention museum at Depot Park, Thursday through Sunday.

    811 S Main St, Gainesville, FL

  • Florida Museum of Natural History

    13 min drive

    Fossils, Florida wildlife and the Butterfly Rainforest. Scheduled to reopen November 23, 2026, after renovation.

    3215 Hull Rd, Gainesville, FL

  • Harn Museum of Art

    13 min drive

    Free UF art museum next door to the natural history museum. Open late on Thursdays.

    3259 Hull Rd, Gainesville, FL

  • UF Bat Houses at Lake Alice

    10 min drive

    Free. Thousands of bats fly out about 15 to 20 minutes after sunset on warm, calm evenings.

    Museum Rd, UF campus

  • Sweetwater Wetlands Park

    8 min drive

    Boardwalk through a restored wetland: birds and alligators. $5 per car.

    325 SW Williston Rd, Gainesville, FL

  • Paynes Prairie Preserve State Park

    22 min drive

    Wide-open prairie with wild bison, horses and alligators.

    100 Savannah Blvd, Micanopy

  • Devil's Millhopper

    15 min drive

    A 120-foot sinkhole with a boardwalk of 132 steps down into it.

    4732 Millhopper Rd, Gainesville, FL

Groceries and pharmacies

For stocking the kitchen and anything you forgot.

  • Publix

    3 min drive

    The nearest full supermarket, with a pharmacy.

    1302 N Main St, Gainesville, FL

  • Ward's Supermarket

    5 min drive

    Independent family grocer since 1951: local produce and a meat counter.

    515 NW 23rd Ave, Gainesville, FL

  • Walgreens

    5 min drive

    Store open until midnight; pharmacy until 9 pm on weekdays.

    1615 NW 13th St, Gainesville, FL

  • CVS Pharmacy (24 hours)

    6 min drive

    Listed as open 24 hours. Call ahead at 352-336-3383.

    1621 SW 13th St, Gainesville, FL

Times are from our front door and run longer on game days. Hours change, so check before you go. Last checked October 2026.
