Why this direction

Character comes from the subject’s own objects

Round two followed the luxury-hotel template: thin serif, navy bands, full-bleed photos. It looked expensive and anonymous. I loaded 27 hospitality and heritage sites in a real browser. The ones with character all build from objects only their subject would have: engraved ornaments, tickets, seals, textured paper, period type.

This house has a lot of its own: an 1899 cabinet-card photograph, a general’s portrait, fish-scale shingles, a porch railing, and a college town full of pennants, ticket stubs and game-day programs. So the direction is a Victorian souvenir program crossed with a vintage game-day program. Below are the sites that shaped it and what each one taught.

  • Everything fast on phones, with the reduced-motion fallback and accessibility fixes already done.

What would make it even better

  1. Original, full-size photos from Craig and Jenn. The exterior is only 1,551 pixels wide, so it looks soft on large screens.
  2. A firm opening date (it’s already late October 2026).
  3. A logo pick from the ChatGPT concepts. The seal and badge shapes here are ready to hold it.
  4. Any vintage UF-era ephemera they own: old tickets, programs, postcards of Gainesville. These are gold for this direction. No UF logos.
